## Answer

The Chisel RoCC hardware accelerator (`MemcpyRoCC`) has been implemented in `generators/chipyard/src/main/scala/example/MemcpyRoCC.scala` and attached to `FastRTLSimRocketConfig` via `WithMemcpyRoCC` in `generators/chipyard/src/main/scala/config/fragments/RoCCFragments.scala` and `generators/chipyard/src/main/scala/config/RocketConfigs.scala`.

### Key Hardware Architecture Improvements over `iter_1`

1. **Direct Coherent TileLink DMA via `atlNode`**:
   - Replaces `iter_1`'s serialized word-by-word `SimpleHellaCacheIF` (`io.mem`) state machine with a `TLClientNode` (`nReadIds = 4`, `nWriteIds = 4`) connected to `atlNode` (on the SystemBus in front of the inclusive L2 cache).
   - The SiFive Inclusive L2 Cache automatically maintains coherence with the Rocket core's L1 D$ (probing dirty lines in `src` or `dst` and invalidating stale L1 D$ lines on writes) while avoiding the 3-entry `SimpleHellaCacheIF` replay queue bottleneck, `pstore` structural hazards, and 32 KiB L1 D$ thrashing on 16 KiB copies.

2. **64-Byte Cache-Line Bursts + Sub-Block Transfers**:
   - Issues 64B (`lgSize = 6`, 8-beat burst) TileLink `Get` and `PutFullData` transactions whenever aligned to a 64B boundary with sufficient remaining bytes, streaming at 8 bytes/cycle on the TileLink A and D channels concurrently.
   - Issues 8B (`lgSize = 3`) `Get`, `PutFullData`, and byte-masked `PutPartialData` transactions to seamlessly step up to 64B cache-line boundaries and handle arbitrary unaligned heads, tails, and small copies without extra round-trips.

3. **128-Byte Circular Byte Stream FIFO with Out-of-Order Read Completion**:
   - A 128-byte circular buffer with per-byte validity tracking (`buf_valid`) decouples source alignment from destination alignment, absorbing arbitrary byte offsets (`src_off != dst_off`) and allowing up to 4 concurrent read and 4 concurrent write transactions in flight simultaneously.

4. **Single-Instruction Handshake & Scoreboard Completion**:
   - Decodes `src = rs1[31:0]`, `dst = rs2[31:0]`, and `len = rs2[63:32]` from a single `custom0` (`funct = 0`) command and returns `1` on `io.resp` (`rd = cmd.bits.inst.rd`) only after all read and write TileLink transactions have received their `AccessAckData`/`AccessAck` responses on channel D.
